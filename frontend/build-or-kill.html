Frequently Asked

How long does it take to validate an idea? ▼

Most idea validations complete in 8-15 minutes. The AI interrogates your concept from multiple angles—market fit, technical feasibility, viability score, and more. The longer you spend, the deeper the validation.

What if Build or Kill says "Kill"? ▼

That's the whole point. Better to find fatal flaws now than after months of building. Our "Kill" recommendation comes with specific reasons—market saturation, technical constraints, timing issues. Many users iterate their idea and resubmit after addressing the feedback.

Can I use this for early ideas (really rough)? ▼

Absolutely. Early-stage ideas often get the most value—the AI helps you flesh out your thinking. Even a vague concept like "an app that does X" benefits from structured interrogation. You'll walk out with clarity on what you need to figure out.

Who is this for? Do I need to be technical? ▼

Anyone with an idea. Students, developers, founders, designers, marketers—everyone. You don't need to be technical. The AI handles the validation and generates execution plans that work for any skill level.

What happens after I get my output? ▼

You get everything: validated concept, roadmap, research, content kit, and a personalized learning path. Most users start building immediately. We also offer optional integration with project management tools to sync your roadmap into your workflow.

Is my idea confidential? ▼

100% confidential. Your ideas never train our model, never get shared, and never appear in promotional materials. We take privacy seriously—your competitive advantage stays with you.
